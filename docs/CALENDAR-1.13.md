# Calendar editing in Daymark 1.13

- Enter the start and end time directly. Whole minutes are supported, including 7:05 and 7:15. The grid keeps its ten-minute guides, and blocks sit at their exact positions. Midnight at the end of a block means the end of that day. Dragging still snaps to ten-minute guides.
- **Edit or delete similar** supports moving the start, setting the end time, and setting a final date for matching repeating schedules. Select a scope, review the count, and confirm. **Entire repeating series** also updates the occurrence you opened, even if you previously edited that date individually. Other individually edited exceptions retain their changes.
- New and existing tasks use one shared color. When entering a custom task, type the name first and pick its color underneath. The color applies to every block using that task. Short block titles shrink to fit; hovering shows the full title and exact times.
- In Week, drag a block's left or right edge across dates to copy its task, time and notes onto each intervening day. The copies allow overlap and are independent of the original. Identical blocks are skipped, and one Undo reverses the drag. Use **Repeat every week** for an ongoing schedule. Dragging the middle still moves one occurrence; the top and bottom edges resize it.
- A Month date or empty cell opens that day directly. Clicking a date heading in Day or Week opens its template and deletion actions. Holiday symbols appear at the right of Month date headings. The current clock is at the right of the navigation bar.
- **View → Week starting on selected date** shows seven days from the chosen date. **Month starting on selected date** shows the range up to the corresponding date next month, exclusive. The same commands are in the date-heading menu. Previous and Next retain the chosen day number across short months (January 31 → February 28 → March 31). The regular Week and Month buttons return to the calendar-aligned views.
- Templates start on the selected date. **Use following day** targets the next calendar date, including weekends. Date ranges initially use your configured working weekdays. Selecting a single weekend or holiday explicitly still works. The template builder also accepts midnight as an end time. The preview lists the target dates before you apply.
- Shift-click to toggle individual blocks or Shift-drag across several. The selection toolbar lets you delete the selected occurrences, matches at the same task/title/weekday/time, everything on those weekdays, or the same task categories across dates. Shift-drag again to deselect. The confirmation gives counts and the affected date range, including schedules with no end date. **Delete blocks on this date** removes one day's entries while preserving the other dates of repeating schedules. Planner Undo restores the batch.
- **Settings → Appearance** includes Charcoal with warm orange accents. The gray, blue, pink and red palettes now color more of the surrounding calendar. Appearance remains a local preference.

The Windows installer is a personal unsigned development build. Before upgrading a working planner, save a backup under **Data → Backup** and use **File → Quit**. Planners without new native minute-precision entries remain compatible with schema 9. Saving a native block, repeating schedule or template with off-grid times upgrades the planner to schema 10. Use this reviewed 1.13 build or later to reopen it. Older builds will report that a newer version is needed. Imported calendar times already supported by schema 9 do not trigger the upgrade.
